How letter grades count

Saved letters convert to percentages: A+ 98, A 95, A− 92, B+ 88, B 85, B− 82, C+ 78, C 75, C− 72, D+ 68, D 65, D− 62, and F 50. Other labels aren’t included. The final letter uses the standard plus/minus percentage bands.
